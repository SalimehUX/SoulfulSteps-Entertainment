Our Story

Our partnership started on the night of one of the biggest salsa competitions in Toronto, at a salsa club called 6 Degrees at Yonge and Eglinton.

I, Salimeh, went to Alberto and said: "Hi Alberto (the dancer I'm meeting for the 3rd time), have you heard about the salsa competition that's happening tonight?"

Alberto: "Yes, I heard." (The charismatic dancer I'm meeting for the 3rd time.)

Salimeh: "Well, great, because you and I are going to be competing."

And that was our beginning…
