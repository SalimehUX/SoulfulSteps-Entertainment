Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you fill out our intake form, we collect the information you provide directly: your email address, phone number, event details, how you heard about us, your budget range, and your preferred contact time.

How we use it

We use this information only to follow up with you about booking a Soulful Steps Entertainment performance or lesson package. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the Soulful Steps Entertainment team. We do not use a third-party marketing or CRM platform to process your information.

Contacting you

After you submit the intake form, a member of our team will follow up by phone during your preferred contact time to discuss your event and customize your booking.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your information at any time by contacting us directly through the intake form or our Instagram page.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out through our Intake Form or message us on Instagram.
